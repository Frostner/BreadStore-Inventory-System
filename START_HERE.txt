BREADSTORE COMPLETE — BOTH WEBSITES IN ONE FOLDER
================================================
1. On Windows, double-click START_BREADSTORE.bat.
2. Wait for "Application startup complete" and keep that window running.
3. Open ADMIN:    http://127.0.0.1:8000/
         CUSTOMER: http://127.0.0.1:8000/shop
4. If starting fresh, look for your one-time admin login in the black window.
   If your old database exists at the previous D: drive path, it is copied
   automatically and your old admin login should still work.

For details, read READ_ME_FIRST.txt.
